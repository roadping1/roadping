 #  RoadPing : Privacy Policy 

 **Last Updated:** May 30, 2026

 RoadPing ("we", "us", or "our") provides a real-time road safety and hazard awareness service. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is
 collected, used, and safeguarded.

 ## 1. Critical Disclosures: Background Location
 RoadPing's core functionality relies on monitoring your position relative to road hazards.
 *   **Collection:** We collect precise location data even when the app is closed, in the background, or the screen is off.
 *   **Purpose:** To provide audio and visual alerts for upcoming potholes and hazards while you are driving or using other navigation applications.
 *   **Storage:** Your real-time location "breadcrumb" is processed locally on your device for alert logic. Only reported hazard locations are stored
 permanently on our servers.

 ## 2. Information We Collect
 *   **Account Information:** When you use Google Sign-In, we receive your email, name, and profile picture URL. This is used solely for account
 management and to attribute hazard reports.
 *   **User-Generated Content:** Photos and GPS coordinates you submit via the "Report" feature are stored in our secure database (Supabase) to be
 shared with the community.
 *   **Device & Analytics Data:** We use Firebase Analytics to collect non-identifying information such as device model, OS version, GPS signal
 reliability, and app performance metrics.

 ## 3. How We Share Information
 *   **Community Safety:** Hazard coordinates and photos are shared with other users of the App to improve road safety.
 *   **Service Providers:** We share data with Supabase (Storage/Auth) and Google (Analytics/Maps) to the extent necessary to provide the service.
 *   **No Sale of Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

 ## 4. Data Security
 We implement industrial-grade security measures, including:
 *   **Row-Level Security (RLS):** Your personal data is protected at the database level.
 *   **Encrypted Transit:** All data between the app and our servers is sent via HTTPS/SSL.
 *   **Signed URLs:** Images stored in our cloud are served via time-limited, secure links.

 ## 5. Your Rights and Data Deletion
 You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information.
 *   **In-App Deletion:** You can use the "Delete Account" button in the System Config menu to permanently erase your profile and personal data from
 our systems.
 *   **Community Data:** Once a hazard is reported, the location data remains in the public database for safety reasons, but it will no longer be
 linked to your deleted account.

 ## 6. Children’s Privacy
 RoadPing does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal
 information, we will delete it immediately.

 ## 7. Changes to This Policy
 We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy within the App and updating the "Last Updated"
 date.
