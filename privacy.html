Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2024. Your privacy is our priority.

01

Data Collection

RoadPing collects minimal data required to provide real-time road hazard alerts. This includes your device's location when the app is active or running in the background.

02

Data Usage

Your real-time location is processed on-device to calculate proximity to hazards. We do not store your location history on our servers. Only the hazards you explicitly report (coordinates, type, and optional photo) are shared with the community.

03

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Reported hazard data is shared anonymously with other RoadPing users to improve road safety for everyone.

04

Your Choices

You can manage location permissions through your device settings. If you choose to stop sharing location, the app will not be able to provide real-time alerts.
