#  RoadPing: You Report. We Alert.

**RoadPing** is an community-driven road safety application designed to eliminate the "invisible hazards" of driving. Born from the chaotic roads of Bangalore, it uses a real-time **Sentinel EKF Radar Engine** to provide sub-second audio and visual alerts for potholes, road hazards, and speed breakers before you even see them.

##  Key Features

- **Industrial Radar Engine**: High-precision hazard tracking using Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) logic for 0ms lag.
- **Background Protection**: Runs silently while you use Google Maps or Waze, providing voice alerts even when the screen is off.
- **Community Reporting**: Single-tap hazard reporting with photo evidence and GPS precision.
- **Jolt Sensitivity**: Automated "Jerk Detection" that suggests reporting when your phone's accelerometer detects a hard impact.
- **Forever Free & Private**: No ads. No data selling. Your real-time location is never stored—only the hazards you report.

##  How to Use

### 1. The Setup
- **Sign In**: Secure Google Auth login.
- **Permissions**: Grant "Always Allow" location access. This is required so the Radar can warn you while you use other apps (like Music or Navigation).

### 2. Driving Mode
- Open the app and tap **"Start Radar"**.
- Minimize the app. RoadPing will continue to monitor your path in the background.
- When you approach a hazard, you will hear a **Voice Alert**: *"Caution: Pothole 50 meters ahead."*

### 3. Reporting a Hazard
- If you see a new pothole or speed breaker, tap the **Large Orange Report Button**.
- (Optional) Snap a photo to help the community visualize the danger.
- The hazard is instantly synced to all nearby drivers.
