The Origin

Why RoadPing Exists

Bangalore's roads are legendary — not for their smoothness, but for the army of potholes, surprise speed breakers, and flooded craters that appear overnight. After one too many rim-bending encounters, the question became obvious: why can't drivers warn each other?

RoadPing was built to answer that question. Not as a commercial product, not as a startup pitch — but as a genuine solution to a real problem faced by millions of daily commuters.

🕳️

The Problem

Potholes, flooded roads, and unmarked speed breakers cause thousands of accidents and crores in vehicle damage every year across Indian cities alone.

💡

The Insight

Drivers who've already hit a hazard are the best early-warning system. All they need is a simple way to share that information with drivers 30 seconds behind them.

🛰️

The Technology

A radar engine using Extended Kalman Filter mathematics — the same algorithm used in aerospace — adapted to predict when a driver is approaching a reported hazard.

🤝

The Mission

Build a community where every driver looks out for the next one. No ads, no data selling, no venture capital — just people helping people stay safe.

The least we can do is look out for each other."

The Engine

Sentinel Radar — How It Works

Most hazard apps simply show you a map dot. RoadPing is different — it actively calculates when you are going to arrive at a hazard and fires a voice alert at exactly the right moment.

The Sentinel EKF Radar Engine uses GPS fusion and an Extended Kalman Filter to continuously estimate your position, velocity, and heading. When a reported hazard enters your predicted path, the engine calculates time-to-impact and triggers an alert when you are approximately 40 metres away — enough time to brake comfortably at typical city speeds.

  • Audio First: Alerts play over your music or navigation so you never have to look at the screen
  • Background Active: Works while your phone is in your pocket or running Google Maps or Waze
  • Precision Timing: Tuned to trigger exactly when you need to slow down — not too early, not too late
  • Jolt Detection: Accelerometer monitoring auto-suggests a report when a hard impact is detected

The Commitment

Forever Free. Zero Ads. Zero Compromise.

RoadPing is a solo-developer project built on the belief that road safety should not be paywalled or ad-supported. Here is our permanent commitment to users:

  • The core app will always be free to download and use
  • We will never sell advertising space within the app
  • We will never sell your personal data or location history to any third party
  • Your real-time location is processed on-device only and never stored on our servers
