# About RoadPing

### Born in Bangalore, Built for the World. 🌍
RoadPing was created to solve a simple but dangerous problem: **unseen road hazards.** Whether it's a deep pothole in the rain or a sudden obstacle around a curve, RoadPing gives you the seconds you need to react safely.

### 🛡 The Sentinel Radar Engine
Our proprietary engine uses high-frequency GPS fusion to monitor your path. 
*   **Audio First:** Alerts play over your music or navigation so you never have to look at the screen.
*   **Background Active:** Works while your phone is in your pocket or running Google Maps.
*   **Precision Alerts:** Specifically tuned to trigger exactly when you need to slow down.

### 🤝 Community Driven
Every report you make helps the next driver. By snapping a quick photo of a hazard, you contribute to a live map of road conditions that makes the city safer for everyone.
