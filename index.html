The Problem

The Invisible Hazard

The road is used by everyone. The least we can do is look out for each other. Born from the chaotic roads of Bangalore, RoadPing eliminates surprises.

🌧️

The Flooded Road

When it rains, every puddle looks identical. RoadPing knows if that puddle is a shallow splash or a rim-breaking pit — because a fellow Pinger reported it earlier.

🚛

The Traffic Shadow

Stuck behind a truck? You can't see the road ahead. Our radar warns you 40 meters before a hazard — time to brake safely.

🌙

Night Driving

Headlights only show so much. Our radar alerts you to deep potholes in the dark before your brain even registers the danger.

❤️

Protect What Matters

Whether it's your parents driving home or a kid on a scooter, every report you make is a "heads-up" that could prevent a tragedy.

Capabilities

Built For Real Roads

📡

Sentinel EKF Radar Engine

Industrial-grade hazard tracking using an Extended Kalman Filter — the same math used in aerospace navigation — adapted for road safety. Zero-lag hazard detection means the alert reaches you before your eyes do.

Sub-second latency
🔇

Background Protection

Runs silently while you use Google Maps or Waze. Voice alerts even when the screen is off.

Always-on
🌐

Community Shield

Single-tap reporting with photo evidence and GPS precision. Your alert reaches nearby drivers instantly.

Real-time sync
⚡

Jolt Detection

Accelerometer-based "jerk detection" auto-suggests a report when your phone senses a hard impact.

AI-powered
🔒

Zero Data Collection

No ads. No data selling. Your real-time location is never stored — only the hazards you report.

Get Started

Up & Running in 60 Seconds

01
🔑

Sign In

Secure Google Auth login. Grant "Always Allow" location access — required for background radar alerts while you use other apps.

02
📡

Start Radar

Tap "Start Radar" and minimize the app. RoadPing silently monitors your path. You'll hear: "Caution: Pothole 50 meters ahead."

03
🔶

Report a Hazard

Spot something new? Hit the large orange Report Button. Optional photo. The hazard is instantly synced to all nearby drivers.
