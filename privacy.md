# Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: May 31, 2026**

RoadPing ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the RoadPing mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Location Data
RoadPing collects precise location data to provide real-time road hazard alerts. This data is collected in the background even when the app is closed or not in use to ensure you receive timely warnings while driving. Location data is only shared with our servers when you explicitly submit a hazard report.

### Device Information
We collect information about your mobile device, including the hardware model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and crash reporting.

### User Contributions
When you report a hazard, we collect the location of the hazard and any photo evidence you choose to upload.

## 2. Use of Information
We use the collected information to:
*   Provide, maintain, and improve the RoadPing service.
*   Send you real-time safety alerts.
*   Verify and display road hazard reports to the community.
*   Monitor and analyze usage trends and performance.

## 3. Data Sharing
We share hazard location and photo evidence with other RoadPing users to improve road safety. **We do not sell your personal information to third parties.**

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
We retain your data as long as necessary to provide our services. You can request data deletion by contacting us at [support.roadping@gmail.com](mailto:support.roadping@gmail.com).

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [support.roadping@gmail.com](mailto:support.roadping@gmail.com).
